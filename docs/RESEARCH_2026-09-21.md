# Research note — 2026-09-21

## Macro policy parity

The runtime DEMO gate already refuses new entries during configured high-impact
USD macro blackouts. Historical opportunity replay did not previously apply that
same gate, so research evidence could include trades that the deployed runtime
would never submit.

This change makes the replay causal and policy-equivalent at entry time:

- the versioned 2026 calendar contains historical and future NFP, CPI, GDP/PCE
  and FOMC decision-day safety windows;
- portfolio research and single-strategy MT4 research use the same blackout
  logic as runtime;
- candidates whose next-bar entry falls in an active blackout are rejected as
  `macro_blackout`;
- existing positions are not retrospectively closed by a macro event, matching
  runtime behavior;
- the BLS calendar synchronizer deduplicates releases already present in the
  versioned base calendar.

Official calendar sources used: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics release
calendars, Federal Reserve FOMC meeting calendar, and U.S. Bureau of Economic
Analysis release schedule.

## Remaining limitation and reproducible cost contract

IronFX HST v401 files were inspected: the spread field exists but is zero in the
available BTC/EUR/GBP/XAU/XAG histories. Tick-accurate historical spread cannot
therefore be reconstructed locally.

Historical admission now uses a versioned broker profile instead of the current
live quote. The profile freezes contract/sizing fields and median observed
runtime spread; the existing +25% spread-slippage model remains applied.

This makes admission reproducible without claiming tick-accurate historical
execution.

## 2026-09-21 hypothesis results

The following hypotheses were tested and rejected, with no production threshold
change:

- London opening-range breakout;
- balance-to-expansion trigger;
- BTC US-session filter;
- BTC failed-auction structural significance score;
- BTC spread/stop relaxation 15% -> 16%;
- spread/stop tightening 15% -> 12%.

The 16% relaxation increased trade count but degraded validation and holdout.
The 12% threshold reduced trades sharply and still failed train/holdout.

The production spread/stop ceiling remains 15%.

## Directional pullback resumption — GBPUSD candidate

A new fixed causal hypothesis was tested after rejecting the failed-auction
confirmation, transition-pullback and previous-day-sweep variants.

Contract:

- M15 must already be `directional_expansion`;
- two consecutive M5 bars pull back against the regime;
- the next M5 closes back with the regime beyond the second pullback bar's
  extreme;
- entry on the next M5;
- stop behind the two-bar swing plus 0.10 ATR M5;
- target 2R;
- max holding time 12 M5;
- unchanged 200 EUR / 1% risk / 15% spread-to-stop / macro gates.

Initial rolling-spread screen for GBPUSD appeared positive, but that result was
not reproducible because historical execution used the live spread at refresh
time.

Under the frozen broker profile and fixed historical holdout end, the superseding
GBPUSD result is:

- train: 9 trades, expectancy +0.197R, PF 1.57;
- validation: 8 trades, expectancy +0.438R, PF 2.30;
- holdout: 1 trade, expectancy -0.240R, PF 0.00;
- admission: SHADOW, weakest independent expectancy -0.240R.

Decision: retain the runtime scanner for diagnostics, but PR #31 prevents new
PAPER entries because the frozen weakest expectancy is not positive.


## Compression breakout hypothesis — rejected

A fixed directional M15 / M5 compression-breakout hypothesis was tested:

- three M5 bars with average range < 0.75 ATR M5;
- expansion bar > 1.20 ATR M5;
- close beyond the compression range in the M15 regime direction;
- stop behind the compression box;
- target 2R, horizon 12 M5.

Result: rejected. EURUSD, GBPUSD and BTCUSD were negative in the observed
segments; XAUUSD/XAGUSD remained non-executable under the 200 EUR policy.

No runtime mechanism or threshold was added from this test.

and contract snapshot at the moment of refresh. The same historical strategy
could therefore change admission state without any historical-data change.

  BTCUSD 24.5, EURUSD 0.00010, GBPUSD 0.00011, XAUUSD 0.28, XAGUSD 0.07;

  `2026-09-20T12:53:56+03:00`.

`efdb35d4936d887d3f36cf3f88beb2ab413e72783bb94da427778d44665885a1`.
