# Research note — 2026-09-21

## Macro policy parity

The runtime DEMO gate already refuses new entries during configured high-impact
USD macro blackouts. Historical opportunity replay did not previously apply that
same gate, so research evidence could include trades that the deployed runtime
would never submit.

This change makes the replay causal and policy-equivalent at entry time:

- the versioned 2026 calendar contains historical and future NFP, CPI, GDP/PCE
  and FOMC decision-day safety windows;
- portfolio research and single-strategy MT4 research use the same blackout
  logic as runtime;
- candidates whose next-bar entry falls in an active blackout are rejected as
  `macro_blackout`;
- existing positions are not retrospectively closed by a macro event, matching
  runtime behavior;
- the BLS calendar synchronizer deduplicates releases already present in the
  versioned base calendar.

Official calendar sources used: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics release
calendars, Federal Reserve FOMC meeting calendar, and U.S. Bureau of Economic
Analysis release schedule.

## Observed impact on the frozen research split

Split remains unchanged:

- train: before 2026-07-01;
- validation: 2026-07-01 through 2026-08-31;
- holdout: from 2026-09-01.

Across the current 5-market × 4-mechanism matrix, 168 candidate entries were
identified inside configured macro blackouts. No strategy becomes ACTIVE after
the correction.

Examples from one same-spec old/new replay:

- BTCUSD post-shock validation expectancy: +0.013R -> -0.015R;
- EURUSD directional-transition validation: -0.028R -> +0.012R, while holdout
  deteriorates from -0.122R -> -0.559R;
- XAUUSD failed-auction remains SHADOW and very sparse; validation expectancy
  falls from +0.667R -> +0.250R while holdout remains based on one trade.

These changes are evidence corrections, not strategy tuning.

## Remaining limitation

Historical replay still uses the broker specification/spread snapshot available
at replay time as an execution-cost proxy. Historical spread capture is not yet
available for the full backtest horizon, so research results must not be treated
as tick-accurate execution reconstruction.
