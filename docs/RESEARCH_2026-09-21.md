# Research note — 2026-09-21

## Macro policy parity

The runtime DEMO gate already refuses new entries during configured high-impact
USD macro blackouts. Historical opportunity replay did not previously apply that
same gate, so research evidence could include trades that the deployed runtime
would never submit.

This change makes the replay causal and policy-equivalent at entry time:

- the versioned 2026 calendar contains historical and future NFP, CPI, GDP/PCE
  and FOMC decision-day safety windows;
- portfolio research and single-strategy MT4 research use the same blackout
  logic as runtime;
- candidates whose next-bar entry falls in an active blackout are rejected as
  `macro_blackout`;
- existing positions are not retrospectively closed by a macro event, matching
  runtime behavior;
- the BLS calendar synchronizer deduplicates releases already present in the
  versioned base calendar.

Official calendar sources used: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics release
calendars, Federal Reserve FOMC meeting calendar, and U.S. Bureau of Economic
Analysis release schedule.

## DEMO_COLLECTION experiment contract

The next prospective experiment is not a relaxation of historical admission.
It is an execution-transport experiment on the broker DEMO account for strategies
already accepted for PAPER collection. The economic hypothesis and risk remain
unchanged; only the execution venue changes from simulated PAPER fill tracking
to an isolated MT4 DEMO order.

Eligibility is intentionally narrow: an admission must be SHADOW with
`paper_collection_candidate=true` and must have a currently open executable
PAPER trade. The DEMO order mirrors the PAPER side, lot, structural stop and
target. Macro blackout, 200 EUR reference capital, 1% base risk, 2% absolute
maximum, 3% daily loss, 15% spread/stop and 25% margin limits remain unchanged.

The bridge is MagicNumber-scoped (`560619`). Unrelated broker positions are not
a reason to alter or close them and are excluded from bridge ownership checks.
At most one Trading-New bridge position is allowed until portfolio aggregate
exposure/correlation is explicitly modeled.

A persisted lifecycle makes entry idempotent across worker restarts. The MT4 EA
now supports an explicit close command so any PAPER terminal state, including
TIMEOUT, can close a still-open DEMO ticket. This avoids the unsafe state where
the research model considers a trade finished while the broker position remains
open.

This stage is DEMO evidence collection only. It does not authorize real-money
live trading or imply profitability.


## DEMO transport note — multi-instance MT4 safety

Attaching one DEMO bridge EA per active symbol is operationally supported only
after symbol-scoped routing. This is transport hardening, not an economic
hypothesis change: no admission threshold, strategy geometry, sizing, capital,
spread/stop, macro or daily-loss rule changes.

The bridge now consumes an open/close command only on the chart whose symbol
matches the command, and command processing is serialized terminal-wide to
prevent same-symbol duplicates. DEMO activation remains contingent on runtime
proof of the upgraded bridge; live trading remains disabled.

## 2026-09-22 — Causal Sequence Research v1

The research layer was extended from single-state causal labels to ordered
three-M5 sequences.

A sequence records the causal pattern of the three consecutive M5 states ending
at the decision bar. Its direction is the most recent non-neutral causal side
available inside the sequence.

The first implementation measured whether the future window eventually produced
at least +1.5 ATR in the sequence direction. That metric was useful but too
permissive because it ignored adverse path ordering.

The report was therefore hardened with two all-occurrence metrics:

1. **directional MFE lift**:
   probability of reaching +1.5 ATR in the sequence direction versus the
   asset/split baseline;
2. **first-touch lift**:
   probability that +1.5 ATR is touched before -1.5 ATR, again versus the
   asset/split baseline.

Bars that touch both barriers inside the same M5 are treated as ambiguous and
excluded from first-touch statistics.

This is still a research label, not an execution result. Every surviving
sequence must still be replayed with actual next-bar entry, frozen spread,
position sizing, macro gate and broker constraints.

### Strict first-touch screen

Requiring:

- first-touch success > 50% in train, validation and holdout;
- at least 30 / 10 / 5 first-touch observations in train / validation / holdout;

left only two non-XAG sequences:

- BTCUSD:
  compression_breakout -> directional_displacement ->
  directional_displacement;
- XAUUSD:
  directional_displacement -> structural_extreme ->
  directional_displacement.

XAG had no economic stop interval at 400 EUR and remains excluded regardless of
sequence statistics.
