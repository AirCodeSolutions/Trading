# Research note — 2026-09-21

## Macro policy parity

The runtime DEMO gate already refuses new entries during configured high-impact
USD macro blackouts. Historical opportunity replay did not previously apply that
same gate, so research evidence could include trades that the deployed runtime
would never submit.

This change makes the replay causal and policy-equivalent at entry time:

- the versioned 2026 calendar contains historical and future NFP, CPI, GDP/PCE
  and FOMC decision-day safety windows;
- portfolio research and single-strategy MT4 research use the same blackout
  logic as runtime;
- candidates whose next-bar entry falls in an active blackout are rejected as
  `macro_blackout`;
- existing positions are not retrospectively closed by a macro event, matching
  runtime behavior;
- the BLS calendar synchronizer deduplicates releases already present in the
  versioned base calendar.

Official calendar sources used: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics release
calendars, Federal Reserve FOMC meeting calendar, and U.S. Bureau of Economic
Analysis release schedule.

## Directional pullback resumption — GBPUSD candidate

A new fixed causal hypothesis was tested after rejecting the failed-auction
confirmation, transition-pullback and previous-day-sweep variants.

Contract:

- M15 must already be `directional_expansion`;
- two consecutive M5 bars pull back against the regime;
- the next M5 closes back with the regime beyond the second pullback bar's
  extreme;
- entry on the next M5;
- stop behind the two-bar swing plus 0.10 ATR M5;
- target 2R;
- max holding time 12 M5;
- unchanged 200 EUR / 1% risk / 15% spread-to-stop / macro gates.

Frozen split results for GBPUSD:

- train: 12 trades, expectancy +0.011R, PF 1.03, max DD 3.03R;
- validation: 10 trades, expectancy +0.190R, PF 1.44, max DD 2.64R;
- holdout: 2 trades, expectancy +0.096R, PF 1.81, max DD 0.24R.

This is **not ACTIVE evidence**: holdout sample is very small and train PF remains
below the 1.05 admission floor.

Decision: implement prospective SHADOW collection on GBPUSD only. Do not enable
this mechanism for EURUSD, BTCUSD, XAUUSD or XAGUSD.

## GBP Asia range sweep — fixed candidate

A session-based GBPUSD reversal hypothesis was frozen and replayed with the
versioned research execution model. It is deliberately independent of the M15
regime classifier.

Exact causal contract used by the reproducible experiment:

- build the Asia range from M5 bars opening 02:00 through 09:55
  Europe/Athens; require at least 60 observed M5 bars so data gaps do not create
  a range from a trivial fragment;
- inspect London bars opening from 10:00 through 12:55;
- treat the first qualifying sweep/reclaim of the session as the episode; later
  same-day sweeps are not separate opportunities;
- require an excursion beyond the Asia high/low greater than 0.10 ATR M5;
- require the close to reclaim at least 0.02 ATR inside the swept level;
- require the rejection wick to be at least 35% of the signal bar and close
  location <= 0.50 for upper sweeps / >= 0.50 for lower sweeps;
- enter on the next M5;
- stop 0.15 ATR beyond the sweep extreme;
- target 1.5R; maximum holding period 12 M5;
- apply the unchanged macro blackout, 200 EUR capital, position sizing,
  minimum-lot and 15% spread/stop gates.

Frozen GBPUSD result:

- raw candidates: 85; executable trades: 21;
- execution rejects: 63 spread/stop, 1 minimum-lot risk budget;
- train: 19 trades, expectancy +0.078R, PF 1.15, total +1.48R, max DD 2.26R;
- validation: 2 trades, expectancy +1.017R, PF 99, total +2.03R;
- holdout: 0 trades;
- total: 21 trades, approximately +0.167R expectancy.

Monthly executable evidence remains mixed (February, May and June negative;
March, April and July positive), so this is not ACTIVE evidence. The correct
decision is prospective GBPUSD-only SHADOW/PAPER collection. Train and
validation are both positive, therefore the existing PR #36 policy marks it as
`paper_collection_candidate=true`; DEMO remains forbidden.

The balanced-auction M15 variant produced only four executable observations and
was abandoned as too sparse. The Asia breakout -> retest -> continuation
variant was negative and remains rejected.

Research decision: do not reduce risk, spread/stop limits or evidence thresholds.
Instead, the next policy experiment should test a narrow DEMO path where a
SHADOW already marked `paper_collection_candidate=true` may become DEMO-eligible
only after satisfying the complete prospective qualification. Live execution
remains locked.


## DEMO_COLLECTION experiment contract

The next prospective experiment is not a relaxation of historical admission.
It is an execution-transport experiment on the broker DEMO account for strategies
already accepted for PAPER collection. The economic hypothesis and risk remain
unchanged; only the execution venue changes from simulated PAPER fill tracking
to an isolated MT4 DEMO order.

Eligibility is intentionally narrow: an admission must be SHADOW with
`paper_collection_candidate=true` and must have a currently open executable
PAPER trade. The DEMO order mirrors the PAPER side, lot, structural stop and
target. Macro blackout, 200 EUR reference capital, 1% base risk, 2% absolute
maximum, 3% daily loss, 15% spread/stop and 25% margin limits remain unchanged.

The bridge is MagicNumber-scoped (`560619`). Unrelated broker positions are not
a reason to alter or close them and are excluded from bridge ownership checks.
At most one Trading-New bridge position is allowed until portfolio aggregate
exposure/correlation is explicitly modeled.

A persisted lifecycle makes entry idempotent across worker restarts. The MT4 EA
now supports an explicit close command so any PAPER terminal state, including
TIMEOUT, can close a still-open DEMO ticket. This avoids the unsafe state where
the research model considers a trade finished while the broker position remains
open.

This stage is DEMO evidence collection only. It does not authorize real-money
live trading or imply profitability.


## DEMO transport note — multi-instance MT4 safety

Attaching one DEMO bridge EA per active symbol is operationally supported only
after symbol-scoped routing. This is transport hardening, not an economic
hypothesis change: no admission threshold, strategy geometry, sizing, capital,
spread/stop, macro or daily-loss rule changes.

The bridge now consumes an open/close command only on the chart whose symbol
matches the command, and command processing is serialized terminal-wide to
prevent same-symbol duplicates. DEMO activation remains contingent on runtime
proof of the upgraded bridge; live trading remains disabled.


## DEMO transport note — five-symbol broker metadata

## 2026-09-22 — opportunity throughput diagnosis under 200 EUR

The absence of broker/PAPER trades is not equivalent to an absence of market
signals. With execution explicitly disarmed for development, the SHADOW worker
continued to run 22 scanners over all five retained symbols and emitted 15
signal rows during the morning checkpoint.

Resolved blocked probes illustrate the economics bottleneck:

- BTCUSD directional transition: -1.0R counterfactual; minimum-lot stop loss
  ~2.18 EUR, slightly above the 2 EUR base-risk budget but below the absolute
  4 EUR cap;
- BTCUSD failed auction: -1.0R counterfactual; minimum-lot stop loss ~2.19 EUR;
- XAUUSD post-shock BUY: +1.8R counterfactual; minimum-lot stop loss ~3.24 EUR,
  above base risk but below the absolute cap;
- XAUUSD failed-auction BUY: +1.5R counterfactual, but minimum-lot stop loss
  ~6.16 EUR, above the absolute 4 EUR cap;
- XAGUSD resolved blocked probes in the checkpoint were losses and were
  predominantly rejected because spread consumed too much of the stop distance.

This does **not** justify increasing risk from 1% toward 2%. The 2% figure remains
an absolute maximum, not a target sizing rule. The research need is instead to
measure the full signal funnel and determine whether causal entry/stop geometry
or a different mechanism can improve capital feasibility without degrading
expectancy.

The next tool is therefore observational only: aggregate each detected signal,
its economic block reason, capital required at minimum lot, and the eventual
blocked-probe outcome. Any future trading-logic hypothesis must be evaluated from
that evidence before code changes.
